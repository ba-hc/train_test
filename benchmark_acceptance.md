# Benchmark acceptance checks (derived from the identical original prompt)

## First viewport, desktop
- Warm low-poly indie world; seaside town atmosphere rather than a generic pastel mockup.
- Legible Saltlight Terminus with red-roof homes, lighthouse, dock, trees, warm windows, townsfolk.
- A continuous elevated rail visibly leads into a varied route toward another island; the scene conveys depth and travel.
- Blue-purple dusk sky, stars, substantial cloud forms, azure water.
- Retro dark-green tram is recognizable at first glance; gameplay HUD is readable without dominating the world.

## Gameplay
- W accelerates, S brakes, left/right changes view or fine-tunes; pointer controls work.
- Speed, passengers, conditions, comfort, currency/streak display and update.
- Doors/passenger exchange required at stations; both directions traversable.
- Cloudworks is reachable only from Saltlight while docked; two upgrade steps change tram appearance; exit works.
- Console errors absent and startup failure is understandable.

## Responsive
- At a narrow mobile viewport, controls, station action, speed and comfort remain accessible.

## Measurement
- Same original prompt, same root model/effort/tier, fresh directory.
- Compare requests, tool calls, fresh/cache/output tokens, wall time, compaction, designer usage, and visual/behavioral defects.
